How to play

Start seated. Look comfortably ahead and hold still to calibrate. Keep your head gently level, but don't freeze. Watch the animated hardcover or its top-view marker for sliding. A little counter-tilt can rescue a book that is moving toward the edge.

Normal mode ignores tiny involuntary movements but is a touch more challenging than V4B. Your L/R and F/B steadiness scores (0–100) are running averages based on how far and how fast the book slips along each axis; the total score also rewards keeping it near center. A perfect centered run still earns 10 points per second (15 with Walking's 1.5× bonus). Hard mode is for players who want a challenge. Walking mode includes a little extra tolerance for natural head bob, and awards 1.5× points. It does not detect whether you are actually walking.

For Walking mode, play only in a clear, level, familiar area. Avoid stairs, traffic and obstacles, and keep your attention on the real world. Stop if dizzy or unsteady. The glasses measure head orientation—not actual whole-body balance or fall risk.
