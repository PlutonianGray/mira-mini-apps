Mira PDF Reader

Keep a small PDF library on the phone, search it, and read the selected document on the glasses.

Glasses: waiting
PDFs stay on this device. Text extraction is local and does not upload the document.

Glasses controls

Swipe/scroll down for the next reading screen and up for the previous one. Reading screens are cached on the glasses for instant local navigation: smaller documents can be cached in full, while larger documents keep a rolling nearby window that the phone replenishes when available. Tap hides or restores the small header/footer. Long-press returns to the “choose a document on phone” screen. For simple PDF images, the phone creates a compact green Mira bitmap that you can preview and send to the glasses. Complex, vector-only, or unsupported visuals still use the visual-note card.
