Steer and shoot
Turn your head gently left or right; look up or down to steer. The view banks and the STEER marker moves with your input. Small initial head movements respond a little sooner; your maximum flight movement speed is unchanged. Long press to reset your neutral head position.
Tap to fire. Rapid double taps also fire, with the same cooldown. FIRED confirms a shot left the ship; HIT confirms contact with a rock. Bright streaks show shots moving away.


Plain small rocks take one hit. Diamond-marked medium rocks take two. Large rocks have a bright X and inner ring: they cannot be destroyed—steer around them. Destroyed rocks burst into fading fragments.


Keep moving: holding the same position for about five seconds brings a large rock toward that position, including at the edges. Move sideways or vertically to evade it.


Pause and reach Casa
Swipe backward to pause. Double tap while paused to open Casa. Close Casa, then tap to resume. Resume resets your neutral head position.


Survive an increasingly crowded field
Your ship flies forward automatically. Head movement steers your position through the field; there are no stages to select. As you survive and cover more distance, flight speed rises smoothly to a limit, new asteroid layers arrive more often, and layers increase from one rock to two. Larger rock types also become more common. There are at most twelve active rocks at a time.
New layers begin about 1.2 seconds apart and gradually approach 0.8 seconds apart at the nominal game rate. A rock growing on screen is getting closer. Dodge it before it reaches your ship. Shooting clears smaller rocks; maneuvering is essential for large ones and while your weapons recharge.


Score and best
  • Small rock destroyed: +25. One successful hit.
  • Medium rock destroyed: +45. Two hits; the first hit alone earns no points.
  • Large rock: no destruction points. The X and inner ring mean indestructible.
  • Near miss: +12. Pass close to a rock without touching it. Each rock can award this only once.
  • Survival: +1 per seven distance units. Faster flight covers distance sooner.
IMPACT ends the run and freezes your score. For the first 1.5 seconds, taps and double taps are absorbed so rapid firing cannot open Casa or restart accidentally. Then tap to start a fresh run. BEST tracks your highest score and syncs to this phone for future sessions. Reopen the companion with glasses connected to restore it. Saving is local to this phone, not a cloud account. Scores cap at 999999.


Weapon heat: make every shot count
Each shot that actually leaves the ship adds heat. About fifteen uninterrupted rapid shots will overheat the weapon. Missed taps during cooldown, a full projectile pool, and taps while recharging add no heat. The existing firing cooldown still applies, and only eight shots can be active at once.
The HEAT bar fills as you shoot. Stop firing briefly to let it cool; controlled bursts can prevent a shutdown. When the bar reaches its limit, OVERHEAT and a countdown appear. Shooting is disabled until recharge finishes, but steering, incoming rocks, collisions and scoring continue. Keep dodging! WEAPONS READY confirms you can fire again.


As your ship travels faster, its engines demand more power, reducing the energy available for weapon cooling. Consequently, overheated weapons require increasingly longer recharge periods.

If steering is unclear
Check IMU READY on the glasses. IMU WAIT means head control is unavailable. Hold your head comfortably and hold the ring/touch control for about 0.7 seconds, or use its long-click gesture, to recenter during READY or PLAYING. RECENTERED briefly confirms calibration. A short hold does not calibrate, and releasing the hold does not fire.
Then try a small turn. Moving your head back toward the calibrated neutral position reduces steering. Firing and slow frames do not change that neutral position. Resuming after pause or a Casa interruption, and recovering from unavailable IMU, intentionally establish a fresh neutral position.
