On the glasses: swipe to move focus, tap to act, and hold for context actions (auto-home, undo, cancel, or a new game). Lowercase ranks identify red suits (hearts and diamonds). Suit display can use glyphs (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣) or ASCII letters (S/H/D/C). T is ten.
