Ziggy PDF Reader

Keep a small PDF library on the phone, search it, and read the selected document on the glasses.

Glasses: waiting
PDFs stay on this device. Text extraction is local and does not upload the document.

Document

Tip: If fine text or lines are hard to read, increase glasses brightness.
Reading screens divide each PDF page into sections that fit on the glasses.

Glasses controls

Swipe/scroll down for the next reading screen and up for the previous one. Reading screens are cached on the glasses for instant local navigation. The current SDK limits each message to about 3,500 bytes, so the glasses keep a small rolling window that the phone refills; very small documents may fit entirely. Tap hides or restores the small header/footer. Long-press returns to the “choose a document on phone” screen. For supported PDF images, including many ordinary JPEGs, the phone creates an adaptive compact green Mira bitmap that you can preview and send to the glasses. Common PDF vector diagrams and supported raster images can be shown on the glasses. More complex, masked, or unsupported visuals use the visual-note fallback.
