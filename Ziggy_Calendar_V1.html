How to Set Up & Use Ziggy Calendar

Connecting to iCloud

Ziggy Calendar does not use your normal Apple Account password. It needs an Apple app-specific password.

  1. Go to account.apple.com.
  2. Sign in to your Apple Account.
  3. Open Sign-In and Security.
  4. Select App-Specific Passwords.
  5. Choose Generate an app-specific password.
  6. Name it something like Ziggy Calendar.
  7. Copy the generated password and enter it in the App-specific password field above.

You normally only need to generate this app-specific password once. Ziggy Calendar deliberately does not store it, so you enter the same password again when you start a new session. You do not need to go back to Apple and generate a new one each time. The password stays usable until Apple invalidates it or you revoke it. Changing your main Apple Account password may require a new app-specific password.

Privacy & Security

Ziggy Calendar is read-only. It cannot create, edit, or delete events, and it cannot accept or decline invitations. It does not send scheduling changes to iCloud. The app-specific password exists only in transient phone memory during the session. It is never sent to the glasses and is not saved. Your Apple Account email may be remembered for convenience.

Using Ziggy Calendar

Swipe to move between items, dates, weeks, or months. Tap to open or select. Hold to go back one level.

NEXT shows the next upcoming events. TODAY shows today. TOMORROW shows tomorrow. UPCOMING shows events across future dates.

WEEK shows seven days. Timed appointments sit vertically by approximate time of day, with major time markers. MONTH shows a traditional month. Drill from month to week row to date to day with Tap and Swipe.

DAY shows the events for the selected date. Tap an event to open details. DETAILS shows the title, date, time, all-day status when it applies, calendar, location, and notes. Swipe to move through longer notes. Hold to go back.
